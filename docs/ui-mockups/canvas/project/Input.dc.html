Touch grammar

Tap a crew memberSelects, and centres the view on them without zooming; tapping the selected one again deselects. The portrait rail does the same.
Tap a tile: previewSnaps to the nearest valid tile; the path, eye icon, pips and rings appear. Nothing happens yet.
×2
Same tile again, or ConfirmCommits. Confirm is fixed bottom-right, off the map, so reaching for it can't move the target.
Drag from the selected crewDraws a path. A drag that starts on the crew member is a path; any other drag is a pan.
Drag anywhere else: panA preview stays locked while panning. Changing it takes a clean tap on another tile.
Pinch: zoom in crisp stepsFrom about 11 to 34 tiles high, only at whole-pixel scales. The map button jumps to the whole plot.
Long-press: whyOn a tile: who notices and from where. On an NPC: its cones. On a wall: what opens it. On an action: its name.
Hold to end the turnThe button fills while held. With actions left over, the unused pips on the portraits flash first.
