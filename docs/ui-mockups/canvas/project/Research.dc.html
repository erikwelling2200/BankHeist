Research behind the UI

21 claims survived a 3-voter adversarial check; 4 were refuted. Left: the finding. Right: where it shows up on this canvas.

1Never let colour carry a state alone; add shape, pattern, icon or text, and let players pick key colours. Red against green fails for common colour blindness.XAG 103 · XAG 102 · AbleGamers APX Applied: every unsafe tile carries pips, an eye, a hand or a question mark; hatching keeps one meaning (next world phase); badges are glyph plus colour; a colour-blind preset swaps green and red for blue and magenta.
2HUD text and icons need 4.5:1 contrast, measured against the busiest part of the background; offer a backing plate.XAG 102 Applied: every chip, badge and countdown sits on a dark plate with an ink outline; nothing floats bare over the pixel art.
3Respect the safe area; let art run edge to edge; size the layout from the real viewport, even for a landscape-only game. Safari and the installed PWA report different landscape insets.Apple HIG: Layout Applied: controls sit inside max(8px, env(safe-area-inset-*)); the notch side and home bar are drawn on every frame; the world image is full bleed.
4Pixel art stays crisp only at whole-number scales in device pixels; fractional devicePixelRatio makes some art pixels bigger. Text belongs in HTML.MDN · phaser-pixui Applied: every frame is the 1x render scaled 2x with nearest neighbour; zoom moves in whole steps; all numbers and labels are HTML.
5Mark of the Ninja draws each noise as a ring whose radius is exactly how far it carries: unobtrusive but exact, so players learn the system without failing.Game Developer, Nels Anderson Applied: noise rings are the exact flood-filled tiles, a thin outline with a faint fill, with a labelled ear badge on everyone inside and nothing on anyone outside.
6Into the Breach shows every enemy action a turn ahead, in the world, with no hit percentages.PC Gamer Applied: intent arrows, countdown badges and hatched next-phase tiles; in a firefight only breaches, landed grenades, wedges and sniper lines are shown, matching §13.6.
7XCOM on iOS: tap to preview, then double-tap or a button to confirm. A finger drifting across the map on the way to Confirm changed the destination.Shacknews Applied: Confirm is fixed bottom-right off the map; a drag that doesn't start on the selected crew member only pans; changing the preview takes a clean tap.
8Into the Breach's mobile release redesigned its touch interface for phones; XCOM 2's touch controls were judged better than its console ones. Tapping a unit and then its destination works well on crowded boards.Android Police · TouchArcade Applied: the phone layout is the base; desktop adds hover in place of long-press and key caps.
9XCOM 2's Geoscape gives persistent state fixed regions: objectives top left, the doom clock top centre, resources top right.XCOM wiki Applied: city map: dossier top left, the Squeeze top centre with each complication on its day, money and crew top right.
+Touch targets: Apple 44pt, Material 48dp, WCAG 2.2 24px minimum (44 enhanced). Secondary sources only.touch-target-size summary Applied: every control is at least 44px; Confirm is 120×60. A 16px tile is about 32px at Act zoom, so tiles are never the only way to hit something: taps snap, the tactical map uses big pins, aiming has a loupe.

Not covered by any verified source: design judgement on this canvas

Pan versus path versus pinch (here: a drag from the selected crew member is a path, any other drag pans, two fingers zoom). Overlay budget (here: one active preview at full strength, the rest from the overlay drawer). Loupe and long-press conventions, timeline, threat-ledger and recap layout, type (Pixelify Sans was dropped because its 2 and 5 read as S and 8; the HUD now uses the game's own monospace and system sans), playback speed, planning screens. These should be checked in phone playtests, starting with M1.

Revision: UI at twice the art resolution

World UI is now drawn at 640×360 over the 320×180 art (one UI pixel is half an art pixel): glyphs on an 11–13px grid instead of 5–7px, hairline strokes, muted signal colours and dark plates with thin borders in place of filled stickers. HUD icons moved from a 12px to a 24px grid shown 1:1, and portraits from 24px to 48px with hard side light and four distinct masks. In Phaser this is a second camera, or UI sprites at 0.5 scale on a canvas rendered at 2× the art.
